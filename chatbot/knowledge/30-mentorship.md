# Mentorship and career opportunities

## Pre-training expert sessions

Professionals explain their route from high school to their current work, setbacks, and useful resources. Recorded sessions accompany the curriculum. An employee volunteering does not mean their employer sponsors Ethioware.

## Research Scholars mentors

Mentors support small groups roughly during Weeks 6–12 of the sixteen-week program. The current guide describes about four hours a week: a group call of about two hours and asynchronous draft reviews. Separate individual calls are not part of this mentor commitment. Confirm the exact group size and schedule with the coordinator.

Mentors help with scope, sources, methodology, and early analysis. They encourage learners and give brief feedback rather than rewriting papers. Glow / Grow / Flag means identifying a strength, suggesting improvements, and flagging major structural issues to Ethioware's team.

Telegram supports group communication; drafts and feedback use the LMS. Logistics, access, and scheduling go to the Program Coordinator. Ethioware's team handles final drafts, revision, and publication support.

## Becoming a mentor

Use /support to express interest. The team confirms the commitment, selection process, and arrangements directly. Do not quote stipend amounts.

## Internships and ambassadorship

Selected participants gain practical experience in technical, creative, research, and operational roles with Ethioware or partners. The company profile describes placements of three to six months, with application, interview, agreed responsibilities, and performance review. Availability and terms require confirmation; a course place does not guarantee an internship or employment. Contact info@ethioware.org.

## Certificates

Use the issued certificate URL to verify completion, for example ethioware.org/WI10092516. Contact the team about missing or incorrect certificates. Never disclose another learner's scores or private records.
