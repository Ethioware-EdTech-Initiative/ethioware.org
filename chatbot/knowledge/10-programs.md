# Pre-training programs

## Format and audience

Pre-trainings help learners aged 18 or older exploring their undergraduate direction understand a field before committing to it. Consult /apply or the team for the current intake's academic eligibility.

Each cohort runs for eight weeks: seven curriculum weeks followed by final assessment and graduation in Week 8. Delivery is online through LMS videos, quizzes, Khan Academy work, and recorded expert sessions. These are introductory career-exploration programs, not professional qualifications.

## Shared foundation

Every track starts with Internet Safety: account security, personal information, safe browsing, device security, scams, and encryption. Learners then study track-specific AP-level and introductory material. Khan Academy work is self-paced within assigned deadlines. Expert sessions explain routes into the field and resources for beginners.

## Medicine Basics

Topics include AP Biology and AP Chemistry: cells, biological processes, genetics, chemical structure, reactions, kinetics, and thermodynamics. Career sessions introduce health and science professions.

## Engineering Basics

Topics include AP Calculus, introductory Python, electrical engineering, circuits, signals, and robotics. Expert sessions connect foundations with engineering careers.

## Software Engineering Basics

Topics include AP Computer Science, digital information, the Internet, algorithms, Python, and introductory web development and databases. Learners also encounter Hour of Code and professional career experiences.

## Law Basics

Topics include AP U.S. Government material: democratic foundations, institutions, civil liberties, civil rights, ideologies, and participation. This is educational content, not advice about Ethiopian law or a credential to practise law.

## Completion and next steps

Tracks include quizzes, Khan Academy progress, and a capstone presented at graduation. Completers receive certificates. Recommendation letters and internship opportunities depend on qualification and selection; neither is guaranteed to every participant.

Apply at /apply. Ask the team about combining tracks and workload; do not promise a particular package, discount, or availability. Research Scholars is separate and uses /research-scholars.
