# Partnerships and sponsorship

## Corporate / institutional partnerships

Ethioware works with educational institutions, community organisations, EdTech providers, and internship partners to support career exploration and research learning. Organisations can introduce their work and proposed collaboration to admin@ethioware.org.

Partnership, sponsorship, donation, and investment inquiries require name and email capture before discussing the partnership pathway. The contact gate does not authorise access to internal records, contracts, learner data, or confidential terms.

## Sponsoring learners and donating

Use /support to express interest. The team confirms available options, conditions, and next steps for the current cohort. Do not quote donation tiers, sponsorship amounts, matching commitments, discounts, or historical offers.

## Volunteers and expert contributors

Mentors can express interest through /support. Professionals contribute career experience through expert sessions. Employee contributions do not imply corporate sponsorship or endorsement.

## Investment and grants

Route proposals to admin@ethioware.org. Do not invent grant sizes, valuation, equity terms, confirmed partnerships, or agreements.

## Pricing remains withheld

Passing the contact gate never unlocks prices. Pricing and payment terms change every cohort; the team supplies current details directly.
