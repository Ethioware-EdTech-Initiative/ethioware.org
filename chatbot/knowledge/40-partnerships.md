# Partnerships, Sponsorship, Pricing & Donations

## Sponsoring a learner

Sponsors fund a learner's month in the Research Scholars Program at
**/support** (Sponsor a Learner):

- $25 = one learner's month funded — Ethioware matches every dollar given, up
  to a full month per learner, so a $25 gift becomes a fully-funded month.
- Suggested tiers: $25, $50, $100, or a custom amount. Give more and Ethioware
  matches that too.
- Frequency: one-time or monthly.
- The signup asks for name, email, optional phone, and country/city.
- Ethioware follows up by email within 2–3 days with next steps.

## Volunteering / mentoring

Volunteers apply at **/support** (Become a Mentor), selecting a track (STEM &
Natural Sciences, Social Sciences & Policy, or no preference), sharing a
LinkedIn profile, and confirming they can commit roughly 4 hours a week for
about a month and a half, mid-way through the program.

## Pre-training payment packages

Accepted learners complete a pre-training deposit at **/pay**:

- **Solo Learner** — one learner, one course — $12. Accredited by Ethioware.
- **Duo Pack** (most popular) — two learners in one course, or one learner in
  two courses — $17 (list $22, ~25% off). Accredited by Ethioware.
- **Global Learner** — one learner, two courses — $31 (list $52, ~40% off).
  Accredited by K12.

Payment is by bank deposit to the CBE account listed on /pay; the learner then
records the deposit through the form on that page, entering the transaction ID
from their bank receipt. **Always send people to /pay for the current account
number, prices and cohort start date** — do not quote the account number or a
start date from here, as they change between cohorts. For discount codes
obtained in the past, or any other payment question, the contact is
**@ethiowarecso on Telegram**.

Note: the deposit is separate from what a place on the program costs overall —
Ethioware's published position is that cost depends on partner sponsors and is
communicated to finalists upon acceptance.

## Corporate / institutional partnerships

The published process for a company or organisation:

1. Send an introduction email to **admin@ethioware.org** describing what the
   company does and how Ethioware would be involved.
2. Within 3–5 business days the team responds and schedules a discovery
   virtual meeting.

Existing partners are **K-12** (Gurugram, India) and **Skilledup Life**
(Manchester, United Kingdom), alongside a third partnership — three in total.
Ethioware's stated model is to reach its 200,000-youth goal "through
intentional partnerships with global EdTech providers", so partnership
conversations are central rather than incidental.

## Investment and grant inquiries

Ethioware does not publish separate terms for investment or grant
conversations. Route these the same way as corporate partnerships — an
introduction email to **admin@ethioware.org** — and let the team take it from
there. Do not speculate about terms, equity, valuation, or grant sizes; none of
that is published.

## Who to route people to

- Partnership / investment introductions: **admin@ethioware.org**
- Sponsorship and volunteering: the forms at **/support**
- Payment and discount codes: **@ethiowarecso on Telegram**
- Anything else, or once contact info is captured: **info@ethioware.org** — the
  partnerships/education team follows up directly by email.
