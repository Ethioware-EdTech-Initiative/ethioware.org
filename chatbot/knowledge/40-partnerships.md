# Partnerships, Sponsorship, Pricing & Donations

## Sponsoring a learner

Sponsors fund a learner's month in the Research Scholars Program at
**/support** (Sponsor tab):

- $25 = one learner's month funded — Ethioware matches every dollar given,
  up to a full month per learner, so a $25 gift becomes a fully-funded month.
- Suggested tiers: $25, $50, $100, or a custom amount.
- Frequency: one-time or monthly.

## Volunteering / mentoring

Volunteers apply at **/support** (Volunteer tab), selecting a track (STEM,
social, or no preference) and confirming they can commit the required weekly
time.

## Pre-training payment packages

Accepted learners complete a pre-training deposit at **/pay**:

- **Solo Learner** — one learner, one course — $12
- **Duo Pack** (most popular) — two learners in one course, or one learner in
  two courses — $17 (list $22, ~25% off)
- **Global Learner** — one learner, two courses — $31 (list $52, ~40% off)

Payment is by bank deposit to the CBE account listed on /pay, with a Google
