# Enrollment & Applying

## How to apply

Applicants apply through the form at **/apply**. It's a short, one-question-at-a-time
form that asks for:

1. Full name
2. Email address
3. High school name
4. Citizenship
5. Preferred program (Software Engineering Basics, Engineering Basics, Law
   Basics, or Medicine Basics)
6. Grade (11, 12, Graduated, or University Freshman)
7. Grade/GPA (out of 100 or 4.0)
8. Telegram username
9. Where they heard about Ethioware (LinkedIn, Telegram, Friends, Other)
10. Whether they follow Ethioware on LinkedIn

Applying takes about 3–5 minutes. There is no application fee to apply.
