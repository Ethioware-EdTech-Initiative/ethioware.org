## Mission

Ethioware's goal is to **direct 200,000 African youth to intentionally chosen
careers by 2040**, through partnerships with global EdTech providers. Ethioware
bridges the gap between high school and professional careers by providing
direct sessions with experts — "doers" — from top institutions.

## What Ethioware does

Ethioware provides high-school graduates with direct, anecdotal online sessions
with experts in their field of interest. These sessions are called
**pre-training readiness**: on completing them a learner knows where to start,
with whom to continue, and who to contact for further guidance.

## Approach

The learning path runs: **Interest → Expert Sessions → Practical Assessments →
Skill Acquisition**, supported by directed resources and an attainable target.
Three ideas underpin it:

1. **Motivation** — expert mentors share the path they took, making the road
   ahead motivating to pursue.
2. **Adaptation** — learning from the setbacks of seniors, to better adapt to
   future circumstances.
3. **Execution** — after the pre-training, a learner can make actionable
   decisions about what to learn, where to learn it, and how to make the most
   of that knowledge.
