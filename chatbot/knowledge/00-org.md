# Ethioware EdTech Initiative

## Mission and approach

Ethioware is a board-led education technology organisation based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Its vision is to direct 200,000 African youth to intentionally chosen careers by 2040. It makes expert guidance available to beginners so they can make informed career choices.

Professionals share their career paths, setbacks, working lives, and recommended resources. Career exploration can also help a learner discover that a field is not right for them.

## Programs

- Eight-week online pre-training cohorts: Medicine Basics, Engineering Basics, Software Engineering Basics, and Law Basics.
- Research Scholars: sixteen-week research mentorship for Ethiopian high-school students.
- Expert career sessions, practical projects, and completion certificates.
- Internship and ambassador opportunities, subject to selection and availability.

## Learning platforms

Jiret LMS at https://ethioware.jiret.site hosts videos, quizzes, and enrollment. Khan Academy supports the pre-training curriculum. Telegram is used for announcements and reminders; Ethioware email is the formal channel.
