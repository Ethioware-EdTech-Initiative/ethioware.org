# Research Scholars Program

## Overview

Research Scholars is a sixteen-week research mentorship program for Ethiopian learners aged 18 or older. It develops research skills from foundations to a completed paper, with mentor support, peer learning, and academic writing support. Prior research experience is not the premise of the program. The team confirms current academic eligibility.

## Tracks

- STEM & Natural Sciences: science and technology research questions.
- Social Sciences & Policy: questions about people, education, society, institutions, and policy.

Learners refine interests into feasible, ethical questions with a local context and accessible evidence. Mentors help select methods; the chatbot cannot approve research involving people or sensitive data.

## Four phases

1. Weeks 1–4: research foundations, reading papers, forming questions, evaluating sources, and referencing.
2. Weeks 5–8: proposals, methodology, literature review, and research ethics.
3. Weeks 9–13: collecting or analysing evidence and drafting.
4. Weeks 14–16: revision, publication preparation, and a symposium.

The coordinator confirms assignments and deadlines. Do not repeat internal cohort dates, student names, scores, attendance, or case notes.

## Learning and communication

Materials use the Ethioware LMS. PDFs support low-bandwidth and offline work. Formal tasks and notices use Ethioware email; Telegram carries reminders. The coordinator confirms live sessions, recordings, and any cohort-specific in-person events.

## Research output

The intended output is a completed paper in the Ethioware Research Compendium and a symposium presentation. External journal acceptance, competitions, university admission, and scholarships are not guaranteed.

Visit /research-scholars for information and interest signup. This program does not use /apply. Ask the team for availability and pricing; the chatbot does not quote cohort prices.
